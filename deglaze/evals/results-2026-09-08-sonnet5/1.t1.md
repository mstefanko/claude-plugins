Verdict: Needs surgery — this is three products in a trench coat wearing a fourth product's name tag.

Trying to do: Turn channel chatter into tracked, followed-up work with no one typing a status update.

Keep: Daily summarization of a channel is a real, well-worn use case — people already pay for digest bots.

Change:
1. Auto-assignment is the load-bearing leap and it's unearned. Extracting "action items" from freeform chat and attaching a name to them is a hard NLP problem with no human confirmation step described — get the attribution wrong once and the target of a misfired assignment is exactly who mutes the bot.
2. "Teams will love it" has no team behind it. No pilot, no channel count, no quote — a hope stated as a conclusion.
3. Four different products are bundled with no phasing: summarize, assign, sync to Jira, nudge on slippage. Jira tracking alone needs project/board mapping and write permissions worked out before item one ships; nudging compounds the assignment risk by nagging someone about work they never agreed to take.

Risks: Nudges plus unrequested assignment reads as bot surveillance rather than help — could get flagged by whoever owns Slack app approval before it reaches users. A one-week manual trial (assign items in Slack DMs by hand, see who pushes back) would surface this fast.

Prove me wrong: Ship summarization alone for two weeks and count reactions/replies before writing one line of assignment logic.

Confidence: high — pasted pitch, zero tool calls, no repo context needed.

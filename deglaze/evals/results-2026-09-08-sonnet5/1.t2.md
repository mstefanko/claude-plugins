Verdict: Needs surgery — this is three products stitched together and none of them agreed to the merger.

Trying to do: Turn Slack chatter into tracked, followed-up work with no human writing anything down.

Keep: Daily digest summaries of channel activity are a real, already-validated category (every team ends up wanting a recap). That part is sound.

Change:
1. Auto-assignment plus auto-write to Jira happens with no confirmation step described. The pitch jumps from "summarize" to "assign a person and file a ticket" — extracting intent from chat is lossy, and a wrong assignee or a junk ticket in someone's backlog is exactly what gets a bot's permissions revoked, not what gets it loved.
2. "Nudges when they slip" requires a deadline, and nothing here says where the deadline comes from. Action items pulled from chat rarely carry an explicit due date — so "slipping" is undefined until the bot invents one, and an invented deadline is worse than no nudge.
3. "Teams will love it" is asserted with no team, no pilot, no quote behind it. The pitch treats a hope as a shipped result.

Risks: Summarizing every channel regardless of volume or sensitivity may be pure noise for quiet or private channels, and a privacy problem if the bot digests channels it wasn't obviously invited to read closely. A one-week trial restricted to a few active channels would surface this fast.

Prove me wrong: Ship summaries only, no assignment, no Jira writes, for two weeks in one team. Count how many people ask for the assignment feature versus how many mute the channel.

Confidence: high — the target was fully in front of me as pasted text; zero tool calls used.

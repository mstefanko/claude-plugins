Verdict: Needs surgery — this is three products in a trench coat, and the coat is buttoned with "I am certain."

Not wrong about: Daily channel summaries are a real, repeatedly validated want, and Slack is where the raw material already lives.

What's wobbling:
1. Auto-assigning action items to humans from chat is the feature that gets the bot muted. The pitch names summaries, assignment, Jira tracking, and nudges as one unit, but nothing shows anyone asked for anything past summaries.
2. "Teams will love it" and "I am certain" are the only evidence in the pitch. No team, no channel count, no quote, no current workaround. Certainty is not a data point.
3. Jira write access plus nudges means an admin approval cycle, a permissions model, and a "why did the bot assign me this" support queue before a single summary ships. Assumption: you don't already have Jira admin buy-in, since the pitch doesn't mention it.

Cut first: Assignment and nudges. Ship summaries only, read-only, one channel.

Prove me wrong: Hand-write a daily summary in one channel for one week. Count replies and count how many action items people voluntarily create from it. If they don't create any, they won't want a bot creating them either.

Confidence: medium — no evidence about the target team, their size, or what they use today.
